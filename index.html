QUICKTOOLS · PDF STUDIO

Make PDFs work
for you.

Merge, compress, remove pages, sign, and convert documents with a growing collection of PDF utilities.

🔒 Privacy first: selected files are processed in your browser and are not sent to a QuickTools server. Use copies of important documents and check the output before sharing.

PDF tools

⇄

Merge PDF

Combine two or more PDF documents into one file.

Choose at least two PDF files.
▣

Compress PDF

Try safe PDF re-saving with compact object streams. Results depend on the PDF; scans and embedded images may not shrink much.

Choose a PDF to start.
Browser-based. This first version may not reduce every file's size.
▤

Delete PDF Pages

Remove unwanted pages and save a new PDF.

Choose pages to remove.
✍

Sign PDF

Draw your signature with your finger and add it to the first page of a PDF.

Signature is placed near the bottom of page 1.
This adds a visible signature image; it is not a certificate-backed digital signature.
⇩

Strict PDF Compressor

Try a compact PDF re-save and compare the result against your chosen size limit.

Choose a PDF to start.
This version cannot guarantee the target size, especially for scanned PDFs and embedded images.
▧

Image to PDF

Combine JPG and PNG images into one PDF, with one image per page.

Tip: select images in the order you want them to appear.
Choose one or more JPG or PNG images.
▧

PDF to Image

Convert every page of a PDF into PNG images and download them together in a ZIP file.

Choose a PDF to start.
Pages are rendered in your browser. Large PDFs may take time and memory.
W

Word to PDF

Convert a DOCX document to PDF in your browser using a document preview.

Choose a DOCX file. Formatting may vary.
This browser-only version supports DOCX, not older DOC files. Check the output layout before use.
✓

PDF Signature Validator

Check whether a PDF appears to contain signature fields. This does not cryptographically verify signatures.

Choose a PDF to inspect.
Signature presence is not proof that a signature is valid or trusted.
↕

PDF Page Organizer

Reorder pages by entering the desired page sequence.

Enter every page number once.
⫶

Split PDF

Save selected page ranges as separate PDF files, bundled in a ZIP.

Each range becomes a separate PDF.
🖼

PDF Page Preview

Preview pages from a PDF in your browser and move between pages.

Choose a PDF to preview.
🔐

Protect PDF with Password

Check whether a PDF supports password protection in this browser tool.

Password-encrypted export is not supported by this browser-only version yet.
This tool will not claim to protect the file unless encryption is actually applied.
↻

Rotate PDF

Rotate all pages or selected pages by 90, 180, or 270 degrees.

Choose a PDF to start.
№

Add Page Numbers

Add a simple numbered footer to every page of a PDF.

Numbers are placed at the bottom center.
▨

Watermark PDF

Add a light text watermark to each page, such as DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL.

Watermark is placed diagonally on each page.
ⓘ

PDF Information

Inspect the file size, page count, and available document metadata.

Choose a PDF to inspect.
✂

Extract PDF Pages

Save selected pages as a new PDF. Example: 1,3,5-7.

Page numbers start at 1.
